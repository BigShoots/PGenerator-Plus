    Before measuring

    • Turn off Dolby Vision IQ, ambient light sensing and dynamic contrast or tone mapping on the TV. They change what the meter reads.
    • Confirm Dolby Vision by the TV’s DV picture mode or badge. Some TVs show “HDR OFF” in their info overlay while DV is active.
