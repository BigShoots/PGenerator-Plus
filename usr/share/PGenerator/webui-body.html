Full Auto Cal
Resets the active LG greyscale DDC state and 3D LUT baseline, then runs the full calibration in one pass:
  1. Greyscale AutoCal, top and body first, then shadows low‑to‑high.
  2. Colour‑only 3D LUT AutoCal, with probe‑gated upload to the TV.
  3. Optional cleanup, after the 3D LUT so the first greyscale pass stays fast.
